# Naming shortlist

All availability remains unverified.

1. **Morrow** — selected. Emotional, future-facing, broad, elegant; possible confusion with surnames and existing companies requires clearance.
2. **Woven** — immediately communicates connection and craft; descriptive and likely crowded.
3. **Rove** — active, creative, internationally concise; less directly connected to finishing.
4. **Lantern** — warm guidance and memorable imagery; widely used across categories.
5. **Veya** — distinctive and pronounceable; invented-name feel needs testing.
6. **Everline** — continuity and longevity; slightly productized.
7. **Firstlight** — hopeful and cinematic; common phrase and likely crowded.
8. **Elsewhere** — imaginative and editorial; long and semantically broad.
9. **Marrow** — depth and essence; bodily association can feel dark.
10. **Cadence** — voice and rhythm; common in music and software.

Next step before launch: trademark clearance in relevant classes and markets, company-name and domain review, native-speaker screening in English, Dutch, German, Spanish, and Portuguese, and confusion testing with publishing and writing software.
