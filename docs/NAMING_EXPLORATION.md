## Selection

Asterism wins as the working brand because it carries the core connected-pattern metaphor without limiting the product to novels, outlines, panels, or AI. It can support a precise visual grammar of nodes, orbits, paths, signals, and revealed constellations. “Causal Ink” scored equally but reads more like a feature or editorial method. “Threadlight” is the strongest approachable alternative. “Arcledger” is the strongest rational/technical alternative.

Before commercial launch, run professional trademark clearance, company-register review, domain and handle checks, pronunciation tests in all supported languages, and confusion testing against writing, publishing, design, astronomy, and AI software.
