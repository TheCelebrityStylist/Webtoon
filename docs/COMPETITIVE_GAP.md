# Competitive gap

Research checked 2026-07-14. Only linked first-party claims are treated as verified. Foretelling did not expose sufficiently specific first-party product documentation in the research results, so its detailed capabilities remain **unverified** and are not asserted here.

## Verified baseline

| Area | Verified capability | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Story planning / worldbuilding / timelines | Campfire describes modular manuscript, character, outline, map, culture, species, language, religion, and timeline tools. | [Campfire](https://campfirewriting.com/write/fantasy) |
| Publishing / audience | WEBTOON CANVAS describes self-publishing, audience growth, and creator monetization. | [CANVAS](https://www.webtoons.com/en/creators101/webtoon-canvas) |
| Analytics | CANVAS documents daily/weekly/monthly page-view and subscriber views plus episode page views, Super Likes, and comments. | [Dashboard FAQ](https://webtooncanvas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/48452348349332) |
| Monetization | WEBTOON documents ad revenue share and Super Like programs. | [CANVAS terms notice](https://www.webtoons.com/en/notice/detail?noticeNo=3222) |

**Unverified:** competitor-level knowledge-state tracking, deterministic continuity, canon-cited AI, webtoon panel production, production approvals, Google Sheets round trips, creator-owned complete exports, and analytics mapped back to narrative entities. Absence of verification is not a claim that a competitor lacks a feature.

## Material workflow advantages to build

1. One graph from premise through reader response instead of separate planning and hosting records.
2. Character knowledge and state at a precise scene/episode point.
3. Deterministic continuity findings that cite both conflicting records.
4. Creator-approved canon as an explicit authority boundary for AI suggestions.
5. Webtoon-native beat-to-panel planning and mobile pacing preview.
6. Panel asset validation, sequencing, review, assignments, and approvals together.
7. Publication versions that prevent silent edits to released canon.
8. Entitlement-aware publishing and creator-attributable ledger entries.
9. Reader drop-off mapped back to panel ranges and episode planning.
10. Manual Google Sheets import/export with preview, row validation, and durable reports.
11. Portable JSON/CSV/Markdown and production manifests under creator control.
12. Reader-response experiments feeding the next episode without automatically changing canon.

The design, wording, and flows must be original. This comparison is a product boundary, not a visual reference board.
