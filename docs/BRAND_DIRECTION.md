# Brand direction

## Working idea

Morrow is the warm, intelligent writing room that remembers the details while the writer stays inside the story.

Promise: **Everything you need to build, write, and finish a story that stays consistent.**

Homepage expression: **Your whole story. Always in reach.**

## Feeling

Creative, generous, cultured, calm, perceptive, and quietly premium. The product should feel simple before it reveals depth. Technology appears through helpful moments—“Lena still has the key in Chapter 8”—rather than technical nouns.

## Visual language

Warm cream is the reading ground. Plum gives depth without a developer-tool black. Coral is expressive action; butter is gentle attention; lilac and sage distinguish creative modes. Large modern sans-serif typography carries the main promise, with serif used sparingly inside manuscript excerpts. Imagery combines contemporary editorial illustration, writing artifacts, character moments, and elegant product UI.

Motion reveals cause and relief: a note arrives, a scene rearranges, a translation convention changes, or a project grows from a premise. There are no ambient node animations or terminal effects. Reduced motion shows the same states without transition.

## Voice

Use short sentences and writer language: chapter, scene, draft, note, character, promise, voice. Avoid architecture, ledger, deterministic, entity, graph, infrastructure, orchestration, and system unless appearing in technical documentation.
