# Brand exploration

The product should feel like a creative companion and a beautiful room for serious work—not a database, operating system, or technical platform. Its emotional territory is memory, momentum, possibility, care, and the quiet relief of keeping a large story together.

## Decision

Woven, Rove, Marrow, and Lantern score highly but carry stronger category associations or a greater risk of confusion. **Morrow** is selected as the working brand because it expresses continuation and possibility: the next page, the next draft, and the future in which the story is finished. It is not format-specific, technical, or fantasy-coded. Its legal and commercial availability must be professionally cleared before launch.
