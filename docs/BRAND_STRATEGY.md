# Brand strategy

## Working brand

**Asterism** is the working product name. An asterism is a recognizable pattern made by connected stars: a precise metaphor for a story becoming intelligible when scenes, facts, people, promises, and consequences are connected. Trademark, company-name, social-handle, and domain availability are **unverified**. No availability claim is made.

## Position

Asterism is private story-intelligence software for serious writers. It is not a publishing network, reader community, marketplace, generic AI chat box, or automatic book generator. Its category wedge is a living story model beside a professional manuscript: evidence-linked continuity, knowledge, causality, promises, revision impact, and multilingual editorial judgment.

## Audience

The primary audience is a novelist, screenwriter, TV writer, webtoon or manga creator, or game narrative designer managing work whose complexity has outgrown disconnected documents. The secondary audience is a small private story team. Public profiles and reader discovery are outside the product boundary.

## Promise and proof

Promise: **See the whole story. Change it with confidence.**

Proof comes from visible source paths, explicit canon states, stable entity references, versioned manuscript saves, author decisions, separate Google consent, and exports. The product never relies on invented customer logos, metrics, research, quotes, or testimonials.

## Personality

Perceptive, exact, calm under complexity, protective of authorship, and visually alive. The voice avoids breathless AI language. It names evidence, uncertainty, limitations, and the next decision.

## Guardrails

- Never imply AI authorship or silent manuscript mutation.
- Never turn private writing into marketing content.
- Never describe planned integrations as currently working.
- Never claim trademark or domain availability without a documented search.
- Never flatten language differences into translated navigation alone.
