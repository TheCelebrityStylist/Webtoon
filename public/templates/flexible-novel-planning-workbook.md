## 6. Continuity pass

- What does each central character know at this point?
- Where are the important objects?
- Which relationships just changed?
- Which promise still needs escalation or payoff?
- Which world rule constrains this scene?

## 7. Discovery space

Label decisions as fixed, likely, possible, or open. Revisit them when the draft teaches you something better.
